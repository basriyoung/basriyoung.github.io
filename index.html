Professional Summary

Why Hire Me

Proven operational reliability, combined with machine learning research I am taking from paper to production.

I look after the part of IT that is not allowed to fail: servers, networks, databases and statutory reporting. At Perumda Air Minum Kolaka Utara, a regional water utility, I run the operational systems and network. At Universitas Muhammadiyah Kolaka Utara I administer the academic information system and own the institution's submissions to four national platforms: PDDikti, SISTER, SINTA and KIP Kuliah. These are the Indonesian government's higher-education data systems, where records must reconcile exactly and deadlines are fixed by the ministry.

Over the last few years I have moved deliberately toward computer vision and deep learning. My thesis compares several CNN backbone architectures for a face-recognition attendance system: I collected the dataset myself under real lighting conditions, trained and benchmarked the models, and measured both accuracy and inference time on the hardware actually available on site.

That combination is the reason I am worth talking to. I am not a researcher who stops at a notebook, and not an administrator who is uncomfortable with code. I think about deployment, database integration, access control and who maintains the thing in two years, because those are already my job.

New technology is only useful if somebody can keep it running every day without drama.
